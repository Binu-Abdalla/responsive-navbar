---

I am stuck.

Look at my current code and determine what I am trying to accomplish.

Do NOT solve it.

Give me exactly ONE hint.

Use the lowest useful level:

Level 1:
Ask me a question that helps me think.

Level 2:
Give me a small conceptual clue.

Level 3:
Point me toward the relevant HTML/CSS/JavaScript feature.

Level 4:
Describe the structure or pseudocode.

Level 5:
Give a small partial code example.

Do not move to the next level unless I ask.

Keep it concise.
